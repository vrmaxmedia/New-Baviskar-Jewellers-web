Good to Know

Questions, answered

Is your gold jewellery BIS Hallmarked?

Yes. Our gold jewellery is BIS Hallmarked, so the purity is certified and clearly marked on every piece.

How is the price of jewellery calculated?

Price = weight × today's metal rate, plus making charges and GST. You can try it yourself with the Price Estimator above. The final price depends on the design, stones and the rate on the day of purchase.

Where can I check today's gold rate?

Right here on this website — the 24K, 22K, 18K and silver rates are updated daily. You can also call or WhatsApp us for the latest rate.

Can I get a design made especially for me?

Please share a picture or idea with us on WhatsApp or visit the showroom, and our team will guide you on what is possible.

How do I order or enquire?

Tap "Enquire on WhatsApp" on any piece, use the Book a Visit button, call us, or simply visit the showroom in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.
